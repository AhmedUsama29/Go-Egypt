Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Introduction

At Go Egypt, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our website, mobile applications, and services (collectively, the "Platform").

By using Go Egypt, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Personal Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Create an account: Name, email address, password, phone number, date of birth, nationality
  • Make a booking: Passport information, travel dates, accommodation preferences, special requests, emergency contact details
  • Process payments: Credit/debit card information, billing address, payment history
  • Communicate with us: Messages, inquiries, feedback, customer support requests
  • Submit reviews: Ratings, written reviews, photos, videos
  • Participate in promotions: Survey responses, contest entries, marketing preferences

2.2. Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Platform, we automatically collect:

  • Device information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage data: Pages viewed, search queries, click patterns, time spent on pages
  • Location data: GPS coordinates, Wi-Fi access points (with your permission)
  • Cookies and tracking technologies: Session data, preferences, authentication tokens

2.3. Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Social media platforms (if you connect your account)
  • Tour operators and accommodation providers
  • Payment processors and financial institutions
  • Marketing partners and analytics providers
  • Public databases and government agencies (for identity verification)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

3.1. Provide and Improve Our Services

  • Process and confirm your bookings
  • Facilitate communication with tour operators and hotels
  • Send booking confirmations, itineraries, and updates
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Personalize your experience and recommendations
  • Improve Platform functionality and user interface

3.2. Process Payments and Prevent Fraud

  • Process transactions securely
  • Verify your identity
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activities
  • Comply with financial regulations
  • Manage refunds and chargebacks

3.3. Communicate with You

  • Send transactional emails (booking confirmations, receipts)
  • Provide customer support
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Notify you of special offers and promotions
  • Request feedback and reviews

3.4. Legal and Safety Purposes

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect against legal liability
  • Ensure user safety and security
  • Respond to law enforcement requests

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

4.1. Service Providers

  • Tour operators and guides: To fulfill your bookings
  • Hotels and accommodations: To process reservations
  • Payment processors: To handle transactions securely
  • Transportation providers: To arrange transfers and logistics
  • Insurance companies: To provide travel insurance (if applicable)

4.2. Business Partners

  • Marketing and advertising partners
  • Analytics providers
  • Social media platforms
  • Affiliate partners

4.3. Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when required by law or in response to:

  • Court orders or legal processes
  • Government or regulatory requests
  • Law enforcement inquiries
  • Protection of our rights and property
  • Emergency situations involving safety

4.4. Business Transfers

If Go Egypt is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Secure servers: Industry-standard security protocols
  • Access controls: Limited access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular audits: Security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Employee training: Data protection and privacy awareness

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Authenticate your account
  • Analyze Platform usage and performance
  • Deliver personalized content and advertisements
  • Measure marketing campaign effectiveness

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for Platform functionality
  • Performance cookies: Help us improve user experience
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences
  • Advertising cookies: Deliver relevant ads

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect Platform functionality.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

7.1. Access and Portability

  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Transfer your data to another service provider

7.2. Correction and Update

  • Update your account information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Modify your communication preferences

7.3. Deletion

  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Remove specific information from your profile
  • Note: Some information may be retained for legal or business purposes

7.4. Marketing Communications

  • Opt out of marketing emails by clicking "unsubscribe"
  • Manage notification preferences in your account settings
  • Note: You will still receive transactional emails related to your bookings

7.5. Object to Processing

  • Object to certain uses of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Restrict how we use your information

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@goegypt.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

When your data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it. Booking records may be retained for up to 7 years for accounting and legal purposes.

9. International Data Transfers

Go Egypt operates in Egypt and may transfer your data to other countries for processing. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Your continued use of the Platform after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
